# عِمارة.كوم / 3maracom — Brand Strategy

## Positioning
The calm, transparent home for a building's money. Every flat owner sees where the treasury (خزينة العمارة) stands, who paid, and what is next — with no spreadsheets and no arguments.

Pillars: **Trust** (المهنية والشفافية) · **Modern living** (العمران والذكاء الرقمي) · **Comfort** (راحة البال) · **Clarity** (الوضوح والبساطة).

## Logo mark concepts
1. **The Arch-Window (recommended)** — a rounded-top window (a mashrabiya/arch silhouette) containing a small check. Reads as building, home and "paid/verified" in one glyph. Navy shape, terracotta check. Scales to a 32px favicon.
2. **Stacked Floors** — three rounded bars of decreasing width forming an apartment block; the top bar is terracotta (the unit you manage).
3. **Letter ع Roof** — the Arabic letter ع (ʿayn) whose head is drawn as a gabled roof. Most culturally distinctive; needs testing at small sizes.

Lockups: Arabic wordmark "عِمارة.كوم" leads in RTL; "3maracom" in Inter SemiBold sits beside it in LTR. The ".كوم" / ".com" part is rendered in terracotta so the dot reads as the brand's accent. Minimum clear space = height of the check mark. Never place the terracotta mark on navy-900 below 24px (use white mark instead).

## Visual language
- **Navy structure, terracotta action.** Navy carries surfaces, headers and navigation; terracotta is reserved for the one primary action per screen (دفع الاشتراك).
- **Soft geometry:** 12px card radius, 10px control radius, full-round pills for status.
- **Quiet depth:** micro-shadows in light mode, tonal elevation (lighter surfaces) in dark mode.
- **Money is the hero:** treasury balances use the Display type style with tabular numerals; progress bars use success green, never terracotta.
- **Status always has text + icon + color**, never color alone.
- Icons: 1.75px rounded stroke, mirrored in RTL only when directional.

## Voice
Friendly, transparent, organized — a trustworthy neighbour who keeps the books.
| Do | Don't |
|---|---|
| "تم تحصيل ١٢٠٠ ج.م من الشقة ٤" | "Transaction processed successfully" |
| "متبقي ٣ أيام على موعد الاشتراك" | "OVERDUE!!" |
| "أعطال المصعد: تم إبلاغ الفني" | Technical or legal jargon |

Short sentences, plain words, one action per message, numbers always shown with currency and date. Arabic is written first, not translated after.
